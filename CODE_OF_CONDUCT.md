# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive open-source community. Participants should be able to discuss research assumptions, implementation tradeoffs, and failures without harassment or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and avoid overstating experimental results.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and intellectual property.
- Help keep discussions relevant and reproducible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, publication of private data, or other conduct that makes participation unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports should be made privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile.
